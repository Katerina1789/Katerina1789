## Personal Projects

♡ **Biba Buba**  
A private shared space that helps couples stay connected, organized, and emotionally engaged.  
It centralizes communication and planning through a playful gesture shopping cart, a note‑board messaging system, photo and video carousels, countdowns, shared lists, curated date/trip ideas and much more, solving the common problem of scattered apps and giving couples one meaningful place to manage their life together.  
Coming December 2026.

♡ **Code of Yarn**  
A deterministic crochet‑pattern generator that removes guesswork from pattern creation.  
It uses geometric primitives, gauge models, and stitch metadata to produce accurate, scalable patterns tailored to user dimensions and skill level. This solves the challenge of inconsistent or unclear patterns by generating precise, repeatable instructions for beginners, intermediates, and advanced crocheters.  
Scheduled for Spring 2027.

♡ **FocusLotl**  
A calm, underwater‑themed focus timer designed to reduce study fatigue and increase consistency.  
It combines countdown timers, Pomodoro cycles, custom focus/break sets, device notifications, bubble‑pop cues, and soft ambient animations to create an engaging environment that helps users stay on track without stress.  
Launching October 2026.

Two larger projects are in development and will be shared once they’re more concrete.
